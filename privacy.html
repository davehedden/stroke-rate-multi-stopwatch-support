Privacy Policy

Stroke Rate Multi-Stopwatch (Multi-Watch) · Last updated November 6, 2026

In short: Multi-Watch does not collect, send or share any of your information. Everything stays on your device.

What the app stores

The app keeps the following on your device only: your settings, any heats you save (lane names, split times, final times, and any heat name, event and course you type), and a temporary copy of a heat in progress so it can be resumed if the app closes. None of this leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party code that collects data. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Exporting

When you export a heat or lane as a PDF or CSV, the app creates a temporary file and opens the standard iOS share sheet. Where it goes (AirDrop, Mail, Files, a printer, another app) is your choice, and that destination's own privacy policy applies. Temporary export files are deleted by the app after about an hour.

Contacting us

If you email support, we receive your email address and what you write, and we use it only to reply to you.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
