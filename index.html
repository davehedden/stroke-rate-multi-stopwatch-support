Multi-Watch Support

Stroke Rate Multi-Stopwatch for iPhone and iPad

Multi-Watch times several swimmers in one heat. Start all lanes at the same instant, lap and stop each lane, then save the heat and export it.

Need help or have a suggestion?
Email wavmaker11@gmail.com. Please say which phone or iPad you use and which iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Using the app

  • Start All starts every lane at exactly the same moment. Each lane also has its own start for practice.
  • Add :05 (before starting) adds five seconds to every lane, to catch up with a scoreboard that started before you. A lane can also take its own five seconds.
  • Lap records a split. Every second split also shows the last two added together (for example the 100 when lapping every 50).
  • Stop ends a lane. If you stopped by mistake, Resume carries on as if it never stopped.
  • Rate measures stroke rate from your taps. It is for display only and is not saved.
  • Save saves one lane, and Save Heat saves them all into one heat. Open the folder at the bottom to rename heats, add an event and course, and edit lane names.
  • Export (in a saved heat) shares the whole heat or one lane as a PDF or a spreadsheet (CSV).
  • Settings (the gear) flips the buttons for left-handed use, and sets rate units and haptics.

Common questions

Does it keep time when the screen locks or I switch apps?
Yes. Times come from the phone's monotonic clock, which keeps counting while the phone sleeps. The screen also stays on while a lane is running.

I closed the app in the middle of a heat.
When you reopen it, it asks "Resume your unfinished heat?" Choose Resume and running lanes carry on with the time that passed.

Why can't I add or remove lanes?
Lanes are locked while any lane is running, so a stray swipe cannot change the screen mid-heat. The limit is 4 lanes on iPhone and 8 on iPad.

Are the times official?
No. Times are taken directly from the stopwatch and are unofficial.

Is my data backed up or synced?
Saved heats are stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud sync, so delete the app and the heats go with it. Export the ones you want to keep.
